	## What the App Store is

	The App Store lists the applications available on your CHORUS instance - all pre-approved to run inside the secure environment.

	## Add an app

	<ol class="chorus-steps">
		<li>
			<h4>Open the App Store</h4>
			<p>From the dashboard, open the <strong>App Store</strong>. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm label</span></p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Find the tool</h4>
			<p>Browse or search for the app you need.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Add it</h4>
			<p>Add it so it is available to launch in your session. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm add-to-workspace vs session</span></p>
		</li>
	</ol>

	## Requesting a new app

	The tool catalogue grows over time, and new tools can be added on request. If the app you need is not listed, ask the platform team. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm request route</span>
