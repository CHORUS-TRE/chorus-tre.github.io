	## Before you start

	Apps run inside a session, so open a session in your workspace first. If the app you need is not available yet, add it from the <a href="/docs/apps/the-app-store">App Store</a>.

	## Launch an app

	<ol class="chorus-steps">
		<li>
			<h4>Open your session</h4>
			<p>From your workspace, open a running session - or start one.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Launch the app</h4>
			<p>Choose the tool you want and open it inside the session.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Point it at your data</h4>
			<p>Work with the datasets in your workspace - the data stays in place.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Do your analysis</h4>
			<p>Your results are written back into the workspace, under the same governance.</p>
		</li>
	</ol>

	## Getting results out

	Outputs leave the platform only through a controlled export workflow, with review and authorization - so sensitive data cannot leave by accident. See <a href="/docs/security-governance/exporting-results">Exporting results</a>.
