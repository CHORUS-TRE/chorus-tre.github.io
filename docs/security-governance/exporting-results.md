	## Why export is controlled

	Data and results are contained inside the secure environment by default. Controlled export is what lets useful results come out without letting sensitive data leave by accident.

	## The export workflow

	<ol class="chorus-steps">
		<li>
			<h4>Request an export</h4>
			<p>From your workspace, request to export a specific result or output.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Review</h4>
			<p>The request is reviewed against governance rules and checked for sensitive content. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm who reviews</span></p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Authorization</h4>
			<p>An authorized reviewer approves or rejects the export.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Release</h4>
			<p>Once approved, the output is released outside the platform.</p>
		</li>
	</ol>

	## What can leave

	Typically de-identified or derived outputs - such as summary results, figures, or aggregated data - rather than raw sensitive records. Exactly what qualifies depends on the project's governance. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm export criteria</span>
