	## Data controllers

	Every dataset on CHORUS has a designated <strong>data controller</strong> - typically the institution or principal investigator responsible for collecting it. The controller defines the access policy: which projects may use the data, under what conditions, and for how long. Controllers keep full authority over their data at all times, including the right to remove it or restrict its use.

	## How access is granted

	Researchers request access to specific datasets in the context of an approved project. Access is granted by the data controller based on the researcher's role, affiliation, and the scope of the project. Once granted, researchers work within a governed workspace - they can analyze the data but cannot extract, copy, or redistribute it outside the platform.

	## When access ends

	When a project ends or its conditions change, access is removed and the workspace is closed. Every access decision is recorded and auditable.
