	## Standards &amp; frameworks

	- <strong>ISO/IEC 27001</strong> - the information-security principles used by hospitals and research institutions.
	- <strong>GDPR</strong> - designed for European data-protection requirements from the ground up.
	- <strong>Trusted Research Environment frameworks</strong> - aligned with SATRE and DARE UK.
	- <strong>European Health Data Space (EHDS)</strong> - built to operate as a Secure Processing Environment for cross-border health-data access.

	## Why it matters

	Meeting the standards institutions already require means adoption does not start from scratch - the platform speaks the language ethics boards and data governance committees use to assess it.
