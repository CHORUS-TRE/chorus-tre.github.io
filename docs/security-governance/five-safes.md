		# The Five Safes
	</div>

	<span class="chorus-chip">Concept</span>
</div>

<p class="chorus-lead">CHORUS protects sensitive data through five layers of control - the widely used Five Safes framework.</p>

	<div class="chorus-notebox">

		<div>
			<b>In short</b>
			<p>Five layers cover who accesses data, why, where the work happens, how the data is protected, and what is allowed to leave.</p>
		</div>
	</div>

	## The five layers

	- <strong>Safe people</strong> - only authorized, verified users can access the platform.
	- <strong>Safe projects</strong> - data is used only for approved research purposes.
	- <strong>Safe settings</strong> - work happens in secure, isolated environments.
	- <strong>Safe data</strong> - data is de-identified at source and protected throughout its lifecycle.
	- <strong>Safe outputs</strong> - results are reviewed before they leave the platform.

	## How they work together

	The layers are complementary: even if one control were bypassed, the others still apply. Together they mean security is enforced by the system's design rather than relying on individual behavior.
