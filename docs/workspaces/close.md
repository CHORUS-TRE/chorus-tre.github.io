	## When a workspace is closed

	A workspace is closed when its project ends, or when the conditions it was approved under change. Closing it removes access and retires the environment.

	## What happens

	Access is revoked for everyone, and the workspace is closed. The data controller - usually the institution that collected the data - retains authority over the data throughout, and any results were already subject to controlled export while the workspace was active. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm data retention on closure</span>

	## Who can close it

	The **principal investigator** closes a collaborative workspace; instance administrators may also do so under governance. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm who can close</span>
