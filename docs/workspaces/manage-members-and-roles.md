	</div>

<p class="chorus-lead">In a collaborative workspace, the principal investigator manages who is in it and what they can do. Here is how.</p>

<div class="chorus-content">
	<div class="chorus-notebox">
		<span class="chorus-notebox-ic" aria-hidden="true">

		<div>
			<b>In short</b>
			<p>The principal investigator invites people, assigns each a role, and can change or remove them. Roles follow the project's governance; permissions follow the role.</p>
		</div>
	</div>

	## Who manages members

	Managing members is the **principal investigator's** responsibility. Other members work within the access they have been given - they do not add or remove people.

	<div class="chorus-notebox">
		<span class="chorus-notebox-ic" aria-hidden="true">
			<svg width="18" height="18" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round">
				<path d="M12 2 4 6v6c0 5 3.5 8 8 10 4.5-2 8-5 8-10V6z"></path>
			</svg>
		</span>
		<div>
			<b>Membership is governed by the project</b>
			<p>Who can join, and with what role, is set by the project's rules rather than decided ad hoc. See <a href="/docs/security-governance/data-access-model">the data access model</a>.</p>
		</div>
	</div>

	## Invite a member

	<ol class="chorus-steps">
		<li>
			<h4>Open the workspace members</h4>
			<p>In a collaborative workspace, open its <strong>Members</strong> settings. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm label</span></p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Invite by identity</h4>
			<p>Add the person by their institutional identity.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<h4>Assign a role</h4>
			<p>Choose their role - the role sets what they can do.</p>
		</li>
	</ol>

	## Change someone's role

	From the same Members settings, the PI can change a member's role at any time; their permissions update to match.

	## Remove a member

	The PI can remove a member when they leave the project; their access is revoked immediately.

	## What each role can do

	For the full breakdown, see <a href="/docs/workspaces/roles-and-permissions">Roles &amp; permissions</a>.
