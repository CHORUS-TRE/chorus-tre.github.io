	## Signing in

	CHORUS is fully browser-based - there is nothing to install. Sign in with the credentials for your CHORUS instance or community. The first time, you may be asked to accept the terms of use.
	<span class="chorus-draft">exact sign-in varies by deployment</span>

	## What is on the dashboard

	The dashboard is your starting point after signing in. From it you reach the main areas of the platform:
	<span class="chorus-draft">confirm menu labels</span>

	- **Workspaces** - secure project spaces where data, tools, and collaborators come together.
	- **Sessions** - the running environments where your tools execute.
	- **Data** - the datasets you are allowed to access, and where you manage your own.
	- **App Store** - the catalogue of tools you can add to your work.

	## Finding your way around

	Everything stays inside the platform. Personal workspaces are private to you; collaborative ones are shared with a team - switch between them from Workspaces. Recent activity and running sessions appear on the dashboard, so you can pick up where you left off.

	## What is next

	With your bearings, the next step is to create a workspace to hold your data and tools.
