	## Choosing resources

	When you start a session you can choose the computing resources it gets - more memory or processing power for large datasets or heavier analyses. Choosing only what you need keeps resources available for everyone. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm available resource tiers</span>

	## The session lifecycle

	A session is either running or stopped. While it runs, it holds your open tools and consumes resources; a session may also time out after a period of inactivity. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm timeout behavior</span>

	## Stopping a session

	When you are finished, stop the session to free its resources. You can manage running sessions from the dashboard.

	## What persists

	Stopping a session does not lose your work: your data and saved results stay in the workspace, ready for your next session. <span class="chorus-draft">confirm what persists between sessions</span>
