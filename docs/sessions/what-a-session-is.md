<p class="chorus-lead">A session is the running, secure environment where your tools execute. Here is what it is and how it relates to a workspace.</p>

		</div>
	</div>

	## What a session is

	A session gives you access to applications and computing resources inside the secure environment. Your tools run in the session; you interact with them through the browser.

	## Sessions vs workspaces

	A **workspace** is the project space - it holds your data, tools, and collaborators. A **session** is the running environment you open *inside* a workspace to do the actual work. One workspace can have many sessions over its life.

	## What runs inside

	Sessions run pre-approved applications - such as Jupyter, RStudio, VS Code, and domain-specific tools - working on the data in your workspace. Nothing is installed on your machine.

	## Everything stays contained

	All computation happens within the secure perimeter. Data and results stay inside the environment, and every action is logged, so work never leaves the platform uncontrolled.
