# Before you begin

Before using CHORUS.HIP, you need to make sure that the research context, access permissions, and data you intend to work with are appropriate for the environment.

The exact prerequisites depend on the project, institution, and datasets involved, but most users should have a clear research purpose, the necessary institutional approvals, and authorization to access the relevant CHORUS.HIP resources.

> For general information about CHORUS access, identity management, and platform requirements, see the **CHORUS documentation**.

## Define your research context

CHORUS.HIP is organized around research activities rather than unrestricted access to data and tools.

Before starting, you should know whether you are working:

- independently in a private workspace;
- as part of an existing research project;
- with data that you or your institution contribute;
- with a dataset that requires specific access approval.

For collaborative work, the project should already define the scientific objective, participating researchers, and the conditions under which the data can be used.

## Confirm that you have the required approvals

Working with intracerebral EEG, neuroimaging, and related clinical data may require institutional, ethical, or legal approvals before the data can be accessed or used.

Depending on the study, this may include:

- ethics approval;
- institutional authorization;
- data-use or data-transfer agreements;
- approval from the relevant data controller;
- project-specific access authorization.

CHORUS.HIP does not replace these approvals. It provides the environment in which authorized research can be carried out.

## Prepare your data

If you plan to bring data into CHORUS.HIP, prepare it according to the requirements of your project and institution before starting the import process.

For sensitive clinical data, this may include de-identification or pseudonymization, removal of direct identifiers, verification of the files to be transferred, and preparation of the metadata required for the intended workflow.

For iEEG research, organizing data according to BIDS or BIDS-iEEG can simplify later curation, validation, and analysis.

Detailed preparation requirements are described in the relevant **Scientific Workflows**.

## Know what you need to access

Your CHORUS.HIP experience depends on the resources associated with your research activity.

Before you begin, it is useful to know:

- which project you will work in;
- whether you need a private or project workspace;
- which datasets you need;
- which applications are required for your workflow;
- whether additional permissions are needed for specific data or resources.

Not every CHORUS.HIP user has access to the same projects, datasets, or applications.

## What happens next

Once the research context and required authorizations are in place, the next step is to access CHORUS.HIP and locate the workspace associated with your work.

Continue with **Access CHORUS.HIP**.
