	## Signing in

	The platform is fully browser-based - there is nothing to install.
	Go to [thehip.app/login](https://thehip.app/login) and sign in with your EBRAINS account.

	The first time you sign in, you will be asked to accept the terms of use.
	If you have not been accredited yet, start with [Request access](/hip/getting-started/request-access).

	## What's on the dashboard

	The dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in.
	From the top menu you move between the main areas of the platform: <span className="hip-draft">confirm menu labels</span>

	- **Workspaces** - your secure project spaces, where data, tools, and collaborators come together.
	- **Sessions** - the running environments where your tools actually execute.
	- **Data** - the datasets you are allowed to see, and where you manage your own.
	- **App Store** - the catalogue of neuroscience tools you can add to your work.

	## Finding your way around

	Everything you do stays inside the platform.
	Your personal workspace is private to you; collaborative workspaces are shared with the people on a project.
	You can switch between them from Workspaces.

	Your recent activity and running sessions appear on the dashboard, so you can pick up where you left off.

	## What's next

	With your bearings, the next step is to create a workspace to hold your data and tools.
