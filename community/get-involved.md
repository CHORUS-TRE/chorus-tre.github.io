	## Join a community

	Access is granted per community.
	To join CHORUS.HIP, for example, follow its onboarding to request access. [Request access to CHORUS.HIP →](/hip/getting-started/request-access)

	## Deploy your own

	Institutions can deploy a CHORUS instance on their own secure infrastructure and join the federation.
	See [Deploy CHORUS](/docs/deploy-chorus/requirements) for the requirements and installation.

	## Contribute to CHORUS

	CHORUS is open-source and community-driven.
	Explore the code, report issues, and contribute on [GitHub](https://github.com/CHORUS-TRE).

	## Partnerships &amp; contact

	For onboarding, pilot projects, or partnership inquiries, get in touch:

	- CHORUS - [chorus-tre@chuv.ch](mailto:chorus-tre@chuv.ch)
	- CHORUS.HIP - [support@thehip.app](mailto:support@thehip.app)
